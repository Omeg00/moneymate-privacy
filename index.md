MoneyMate — Privacy Policy
Last updated: 31 May 2026 · Version 1.1.0

TL;DR — MoneyMate is 100% offline.
We do not collect, store, share, or transmit any of your data. Everything you enter stays on your device and only on your device. No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking. Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all your data.
1. Who we are
MoneyMate is a personal finance app developed and maintained by an independent developer. Contact: waramwit@gmail.com.

2. What information we collect
None. MoneyMate does not collect any personal or usage information.

All financial entries you make in the app (transactions, categories, asset records, language and currency preferences) are stored locally on your device, in a private app database that no third party can read. Nothing is transmitted to us or to any server.

3. What we do not do
We do not have a backend server. No account, no sign-up, no login.
We do not use analytics, crash reporting, or telemetry SDKs.
We do not show advertisements.
We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third party.
We do not track your location.
We do not access your contacts, camera, photos, microphone, SMS, call logs, calendar, or any other system data.
4. Permissions
MoneyMate requests no runtime permissions. The app only uses the Android system's standard private storage for its local database — this does not require user permission and the data is sandboxed to MoneyMate alone.

If you used MoneyMate v1.0: earlier versions requested photo/storage access for an automatic slip-scanning feature. That feature, and the associated permissions, have been completely removed in v1.1.0. The app no longer reads any photos or files outside its own sandbox.
5. Where your data lives
Your data exists in exactly one place: a local SQLite database inside MoneyMate's private app storage on your phone. It is never copied, mirrored, backed up to our servers, or shared with anyone.

If your device has Android Auto Backup enabled (a system-level Google feature), the operating system may include MoneyMate's data in your encrypted Google Drive device backup — this is controlled by Android itself, not by us. You can disable app backup in Android Settings → System → Backup if you prefer.

6. Children's privacy
MoneyMate is suitable for general audiences. It does not target children, does not knowingly collect any information from anyone (children included), and complies with COPPA by virtue of not collecting data at all.

7. Your rights
Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to give you, delete, or correct on our side. You have full control over your data on your device:

Export / view: use the app's interface to view your records.
Delete: use Settings → "Delete all data" in the app, or uninstall the app. Both actions permanently delete every record from your device.
8. Third-party services
MoneyMate uses no third-party SDKs that collect or transmit data. The only third-party code embedded in the app is open-source Flutter UI libraries used entirely on-device for rendering (charts, date pickers, etc.).

When you download or update MoneyMate, Google Play handles the distribution; that interaction is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, not by us.

9. Changes to this policy
If our practices change in a future version (e.g. if we ever add an optional cloud-sync feature), we will update this page and clearly note the change in the app's release notes. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the most recent revision.

10. Contact
Questions or concerns? Email waramwit@gmail.com.
